STUDENT QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can students from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and the Middle East apply?

International applicants from these regions can enquire about UNITEN programmes. Admission eligibility, documentation and visa requirements depend on the applicant's qualifications and nationality.

What are the tuition fees and scholarship options?

Fees and any available scholarships vary by programme and intake. Contact Overseas Highway for guidance and confirm all final amounts and awards with UNITEN's official information.

Do I need IELTS or another English qualification?

English-language requirements depend on the selected programme and applicable university policies. Ask for an individual eligibility assessment.

Will Overseas Highway issue my university offer or visa?

No. UNITEN controls university admissions and offers. Immigration and relevant Malaysian authorities manage visa decisions. Overseas Highway provides counselling and application support.

Where can I submit an official UNITEN application?

The university's official application system is available at apply.uniten.edu.my/myuniten/. Our team can help explain the steps beforehand.
